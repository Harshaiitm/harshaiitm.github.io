Aerospace Control Systems Lab

Lead Researcher: Harshad Prakash Donawade

Advancing the frontiers of flight dynamics, optimal control, and autonomous aerial robotics.

Research Areas

Flight Control Laws

Development and validation of performance models for high-lift systems and SFCC software upgrades (A350/A380/SA).

Trajectory Optimisation

Utilizing Pseudospectral methods (LG, LGL, LGR, CGL) for fuel-optimal launch vehicle ascent and supersonic climb.

UAV Autonomy

Design and fabrication of hybrid VTOL systems and investigation of propeller downwash in aerial spray systems.

Lab Director / Author

Harshad Prakash Donawade, M.Tech (IITM)

Harshad is a Flight Controls Development Engineer at Airbus India and the primary author behind the lab's research initiatives. With an AIR-356 in GATE Aerospace, his expertise spans flight mechanics, optimal control, and UAV system design.
