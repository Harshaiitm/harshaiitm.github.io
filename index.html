Welcome to ACSL

Our lab focuses on the intersection of mathematical optimization and high-performance flight mechanics. We specialize in Pseudospectral methods for launch vehicles and autonomous UAV trajectory stability.

Research Overview

Trajectory Optimization

Mathematical modeling and fuel-minimization for multi-stage launch vehicles using Pseudospectral collocation methods (LG, LGL, LGR, CGL).

UAV Dynamics

Experimental investigation of propeller downwash influence on spray characteristics using Laser Doppler Velocimetry (LDV).

Get Started

To implement ACSL control laws in your simulation environment:

  1. Clone the ACSL core library.
  2. Initialize MATLAB Optimization Toolbox.
  3. Run /scripts/setup_pseudospectral.m

Example Problems

Goddard's Rocket Problem

Solving for optimal thrust to maximize final altitude under atmospheric drag.

% Cost Function: Maximize Final Altitude
f = @(x,u) [x(2); (u - drag(x(1),x(2)))/m - g; -u/c];
