Welcome to ACSL

Our lab focuses on the intersection of mathematical optimization and high-performance flight mechanics. We specialize in Pseudospectral methods for launch vehicles and autonomous UAV trajectory stability.

Research Overview

Trajectory Optimization

Utilizing GMAT and MATLAB to solve fuel-optimal ascent problems using Legendre-Gauss (LG) and Chebychev-Gauss-Lobatto (CGL) nodes.

Fluid-Structure Interaction

Experimental studies on propeller downwash in UASS using Laser Doppler Velocimetry (LDV).

Getting Started

Follow these steps to integrate ACSL optimization tools into your workflow:

  1. Clone the ACSL Repository from GitHub.
  2. Ensure MATLAB R2022b or later is installed with the Optimization Toolbox.
  3. Set the system path to include the `/src/pseudospectral/` directory.
