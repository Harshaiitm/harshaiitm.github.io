About the Author

D Harshad Prakash is an Aerospace Engineering scholar specializing in Flight Control Systems and Trajectory Optimisation. An alumnus of IIT Madras, his research focuses on bridging the gap between theoretical optimal control and practical autonomous aircraft design.

His contributions include critical research in Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) and the implementation of pseudospectral schemes for launch vehicle ascent mechanics.
